## Second round

Four reviewers on the branch's diff at bae0d3a: files, scales, lifetimes and options,
tests. All four points fixed, each with a check that fails without it.

1. **`-f` followed someone else's link** (confirmed with a probe), a regression of
   point 5. In a shared folder without the sticky bit, where `fs.protected_symlinks`
   doesn't help, a planted `shot.png -> ~/.bashrc` got the PNG, and one to nowhere
   created its target. Now only a link of one's own or root's is followed, or one
   that leads to stdout itself (in a container root's shows as nobody's, as
   /dev/stdout in mwc's box). Any other is replaced. `tests/otheruid.c` makes the
   test's own links look like a stranger's.
2. **Two monitors at the same fractional scale were resampled** (confirmed by the
   numbers), a regression of point 2. 1280x720 and 1920x1080 at 1.3 differ in the y
   scale in the fourth place, so one was stretched by 1.0006 and filtered. An output
   off by less than a pixel over its size is now copied as it is, and the picture's
   size comes from where the outputs lie.
3. **`-h` didn't check its write to stdout**, unlike `-v` and `-l`. As in
   digitalClock and displayOff: checked, and `-h` stands alone.
4. **The option tests reached the running session** (confirmed: mwc's box passes
   `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` and the runtime directory through). A regression in the checks
   would have copied into its clipboard or frozen its screen. The tests now set
   `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` to their own directory and unset `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` before
   anything runs.

## Fourth round

Two reviewers on dd7d062: `-f`, placement. Eight points. Patching each case had opened
new ones, so both parts were rebuilt on a simpler rule instead; all eight are settled,
each case with a check that fails without its part.

`-f` no longer follows links. stdout is written to as fd 1. Otherwise the name is opened
as a path only, `O_PATH`: the kernel follows its links with its own protections, and
nothing waits or wakes a device. A pipe or device of one's own found that way is opened
again through `/proc/self/fd`, so exactly what was checked is written. Anything else is
replaced through the rename. This settles:

1. **`realpath()` bypassed `fs.protected_symlinks`** (confirmed with strace): one's own
   link through someone else's in /tmp led the rename onto ~/.bashrc. No link is
   resolved in userspace any more.
2. **A swapped in pipe nobody reads hung screenShot** (confirmed with a probe). The
   check is on what `O_PATH` found, which never waits; someone else's pipe is replaced.
3. **`-f >(...)` and `-f /dev/fd/3` failed**, a regression (confirmed with a probe).
   They are pipes of one's own and get the picture.
4. **One's own link that could not be resolved was replaced silently.** Links are
   replaced now, as README says.
5. **CAVEATS described links written in place**; rewritten.

Placement goes through one map: every edge is its logical place times the largest
scale, rounded down, and every monitor fills its box, copied as it is when the box is
its size within a pixel, a spare row or column its edge again. This settles:

6. **A blended row beside a monitor just too large to copy 1:1** (confirmed by a
   render): 1920x1200 above 2560x1440 at 1.3.
7. **A black column with three in a row** (confirmed by the numbers): 1920, 1920 and
   2560 wide at 1.5, each place rounded down on its own.
8. **`cut()` a picture pixel off on a monitor scaled up**: it now rounds in the
   monitor's own pixels first, as the overlay does, then scales.
